Comparative market analysis

The problem
A seller needs to know what their home should list for.
What I did
Pulled four sold comps within 1 mile and 90 days, compared size, condition and days on market, and averaged price per sq ft.
What it shows
$262,625 average sale price, $176.08 per sq ft, 24 days on market.

Lead pipeline

The problem
Good prospects get lost when nobody tracks who was contacted.
What I did
Logged prospects from Zillow, Facebook groups and LinkedIn, noted the signal that made each a fit, and tracked their status.
What it shows
A clear daily list of who to contact next and why.

Inbox triage

The problem
